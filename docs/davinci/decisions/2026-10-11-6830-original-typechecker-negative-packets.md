effective program or a genuine native diagnostic disappeared during mapping.

command receipts and provide no original-suite passing credit.

This changes no production code, selected config, baseline, diagnostic
filter, or required check. The job preserves an unsuccessful original oracle
as an unsuccessful result and retains the whole artifact on failure.
Any production repair requires packet-backed ownership coordination and
fresh complete original Actions qualification before queue admission.
